## 0. How A18 works

A18 is where sensing turns into force, and the whole design exists to make that turn narrow, calibrated, reversible and accountable, without letting a powerful offender slip through the gaps. It rests on one governing idea, the sensing-versus-coercion synthesis: the polity may SENSE harm richly across every value frame, but it may COERCE only narrowly, in strength proportional to measurement confidence times cross-frame reach times floor-breach severity. Rich vision never licenses converting a state-frame preference into force.

**The offense and the gate (A18a).** An offense is not new data: it is a classification layered on an already-recorded act (K2) or occurrence (X1) and its anti-value booking (V4), by a closed taxonomy of three kinds. A rights-violation (a breach of a Charter Art. 4 inviolable right) is criminal by kind and can never be priced away; priced anti-value (externalities, negligence) is the priceable class; systemic harm routes to cohort restitution plus, where it breaches an Art. 4 right, the criminal ladder. Whether the state may coerce at all, and how hard, is decided by one computable, versioned, discretion-free threshold function, fail-closed until calibrated, evidence-disciplined on both of its factors (confidence and cross-frame objectivity must each be multi-source, staked, and attested by frames not aligned with the charging organ), and refused entirely on any vague or retroactive offense definition. Statistical attribution may ground cohort restitution and organizational standing loss but never hard coercion of an individual.

**Investigation, the ladder and the outcome (A18b, A18c, A18d).** Investigation is a bounded, authorized, logged process: every evidence read is a judicial access contract through due process, per-subject and necessity-found, never a fishing expedition, with the target advocate-represented from first coercive contact. The enforcement ladder is one canonical closed set (annotation, priced-debit, capability-restriction, standing-loss, custodial-restriction); soft rungs may follow measurement, but no hard rung applies to a person without an adjudicated finding, and a pre-adjudication asset measure can only be a reversible, capped, contest-suspendable freeze, never a punishment. The aim is restorative first: restore every damaged party on every damaged axis, computed from the flow record, with commensurate deterrence that is never revenue and an offender treated as a value transformer to be returned to positive contribution. An inviolable dignity floor, defined once and referenced everywhere, sits outside the calculus as an absolute limit that no offense, no rung and no emergency may ever touch.

**The enforcers and the accused (A18e, A18f).** Enforcement is polycentric with no apex: plural organs, mutually anchored, with A6 courts (outside the enforcement organs) as the neutral conflict resolver. Every enforcement act is itself a K2 act, signed, logged and non-suppressible; coercive force is anti-value unless a specific lawful basis justifies it and is always the loudest audit; the enforcer is never above the ladder and cannot hide behind qualified immunity or a superior's order. The accused gets the full procedural spine: presumption of innocence, a proof standard that rises with the rung (near-certainty for any loss of liberty), the right to see the evidence despite the state's default-deny (with a gisting floor so sealed material is never decisive and a witness-protection channel so disclosure is not weaponized against sources), zero adverse weight from silence in a criminal matter, hard in-text ceilings on detention, protections for children and the incapacitated, appeals of every hard rung, and full restoration for the wrongly coerced, discovery-based and time-unbarred.

**Both flanks, priced.** The specification is written so that a powerful offender cannot buy out a rights violation at the classification seam, launder the criminal class through a dissolving shell, run out the clock by obstruction, engineer a collusive acquittal, or scapegoat a low-capacity patsy, and equally so that the state cannot coerce on a vague pretext, inflate cross-frame claims, punish an offender's associates through the delegation graph, turn deterrence into revenue, or erode the dignity floor by redefinition. A18 that guarded only one flank would be an instrument of the other.

The sections follow: offense and threshold; investigation; the ladder and calibration; penalty and restoration; agents and force; due process and appeals.

## A18 offense: definition, taxonomy and the coercion threshold

### Purpose

This section fixes what counts as an offense, the closed taxonomy of offense kinds, and the single gate that licenses any coercion at all. It is the entry seam of the whole A18 enforcement chain: an occurrence becomes an offense here, acquires a kind here, and is measured against the coercion threshold here before any downstream organ may assemble a penalty. Because both offender impunity and state over-reach are catastrophic on this surface, the section is written to protect both flanks at once: it defines offenses with lex-certa precision so the state cannot coerce on a vague pretext, and it refuses to price away a rights-violation so a powerful offender cannot buy out a floor breach. The gate itself carries no discretion: it is a computable, versioned function, fail-closed until calibrated.

### Object model

- occurrence: an X1-I14 victim-originated or system-originated event naming a candidate actor; input, not yet an offense.
- offense: a classified occurrence carrying an offense_kind, an offense_norm_set reference, and a matter identity.
- offense_kind: closed enum {rights_violation, priced_anti_value, systemic_harm} (A18-I2).
- offense_norm_set: the composed set of A5 offense definitions the conduct is charged under; each member SHALL satisfy the definiteness standard of A18-I7.
- matter: the underlying occurrence or aggregated-flow identity (the composed K3/K5 structure of K2-I32), not the legal characterization; relabeling the same conduct is the same matter.
- confidence_class: the graded, staked, calibration-bound reliability of attribution (A18-I8).
- cross_frame_reach: the graded objectivity of the asserted harm across frames, corroborated by non-aligned frames (A18-I8).
- floor_breach_severity: closed ordered enum {none, cohort_floor, threatened_inviolable, inviolable} (A18-I4).
- coercion_rung: the canonical closed ladder {annotation, priced_debit, capability_restriction, standing_loss, custodial_restriction}; prosecution, where a distinct step, is named explicitly and never folded into custodial_restriction.
- max_rung: the ceiling output of the calibration function f() (A18-I4).
- provisional_freeze: a reversible, magnitude- and duration-capped preservation measure; explicitly NOT the priced_debit rung (A18-I6).
- dignity_floor: the single A9-owned inviolable-floor object, referenced by name, never re-enumerated here (A18-I12).

### Invariants

- A18-I1 (origination and silence). An offense arises only from an occurrence naming an actor; the case-opener does not set the kind, classification does (P2). Once an occurrence is classified offense_kind=rights_violation, the X1-I14 / K2-P4 victim-originated non-response inference is inert for the A18-I4 confidence factor: it MAY sustain the case-opener's survival but SHALL NOT contribute to the coercion confidence_class. Cross-reference the criminal-silence bar in the due-process section.
- A18-I2 (closed taxonomy and routing). Every offense is exactly one of {rights_violation, priced_anti_value, systemic_harm}. Routing is fixed: rights_violation to the criminal in-kind, non-priceable class; priced_anti_value to the priced class; systemic_harm to priced-class cohort restitution, plus, only where it also breaches a Charter Art. 4 right, the criminal ladder. A systemic_harm that breaches a Charter Art. 6 cohort floor routes to priced-class cohort restitution AND licenses the hard ladder through the cohort_floor severity band of A18-I4.
- A18-I3 (non-priceability, no buy-out). rights_violation is non-priceable by kind. A priced settlement purporting to discharge a Charter Art. 4 floor breach is void ab initio and attaches no finality or jeopardy bar regardless of concealment, because the criminal class was never validly adjudicated. Under-classification of an on-record floor breach is an independent, non-concealment reopening ground. The kind determination is independently re-derived by the A6 court and is never inherited from an offender-influenced V4 booking.
- A18-I4 (the coercion threshold). max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity): a monotone, capped, uniform, versioned function computed with NO per-case discretion. floor_breach is BOTH a gate (some floor_breach above none SHALL be present for any hard rung, capability_restriction and above) AND a multiplicand (severity scales the ceiling). floor_breach_severity distinguishes cohort_floor (Charter Art. 6) as a licensing band from inviolable (Charter Art. 4), and adds threatened_inviolable, graded on the confidence and cross-frame reach of the RISK, so attempt and endangerment are gateable without materialized harm. FAIL-CLOSED INTERIM: until A10 ratifies and publishes f(), max_rung is capped at annotation for every finding except objective-corroborated priced-class harm, which MAY reach priced_debit; NO hard rung SHALL issue on an unpublished or unversioned function.
- A18-I5 (no state-frame preference). An intra-frame moral objection to a person's speech, belief or practice SHALL NOT be relabeled as objective cross-frame harm. State-frame preference never supplies cross_frame_reach; the discipline of A18-I8 is the procedural check.
- A18-I6 (presumption of innocence, soft/hard line). No HARD rung (capability_restriction and above) SHALL apply to a person except on an adjudicated A6 finding. Pre-adjudication, the only permitted asset measure is a reversible provisional_freeze: a preservation measure, explicitly NOT the priced_debit rung, magnitude- and duration-capped. Actual forfeiture and any accruing continuous debit require an A6 attested or confirmed finding, or an uncontested attestation after a defined contest window. Contest has SUSPENSIVE effect: accrual against a person freezes on contest until adjudicated, with automatic restoration-with-interest if the finding is not sustained.
- A18-I7 (non-retroactivity and lex certa). No coercion SHALL issue for conduct not defined as an offense in advance (nulla poena sine lege). Every A5 offense definition composing an offense_norm_set SHALL meet a definiteness standard, conduct ascertainable in advance, void-for-vagueness reviewable at A6. Classification and coercion are refused on any offense whose definition fails it.
- A18-I8 (evidentiary discipline, both factors). confidence_class SHALL be multi-source, staked, calibration-bound and revaluable. The same discipline binds cross_frame_reach: the burden of proving cross-frame objectivity is on the asserting organ to the same corroborated standard as confidence; the objectivity SHALL be attested by frames NOT aligned with the charging organ (V5 frame-holders or the affected cohort); the frame determination is independently contestable before any hard rung.
- A18-I9 (no statistical hard coercion of individuals). Purely statistical or market-share attribution MAY ground cohort-level restitution and organization-level standing_loss ONLY. It SHALL NEVER supply the confidence basis for any hard rung against an individual natural person, who requires individualized, non-statistical, objective-corroborated evidence of that person's own contribution.
- A18-I10 (self-report finality, gated concealment). An honest self-report settles finally ONLY to the fully and accurately disclosed scope. Any material omission voids finality for the ENTIRE matter and flips it into the perpetual, no-limitation class, with the burden on the self-reporter to show disclosure was complete. A concealment finding SHALL independently clear the hard-rung confidence threshold (high-confidence, objective, corroborated) before any limitation or finality bar lifts; a below-threshold concealment claim yields at most annotation and does not reopen a settled matter.
- A18-I11 (capacity-mismatch tracing). On any capacity mismatch, where the attributed actor demonstrably lacks the means or capability the harm required, upstream tracing to principals, procurers and beneficial owners is mandatory before closure. A conviction or settlement with one party never bars pursuit of undisclosed principals or procurers on the same occurrence. An uncorroborated admission inconsistent with the attributed actor's demonstrated capacity SHALL NOT be relied upon.
- A18-I12 (inviolable floor, single object). The absolute jus cogens LIMIT is the single A9-owned dignity_floor object; this section references it by name and never re-enumerates it. Any narrower local restatement is void and does not shrink the floor. The dignity_floor is a hard LIMIT outside the coercion calculus entirely and is distinct in role from the licensing floor_breach gate of A18-I4: an Art. 4 inviolable breach is the absolute LIMIT, an Art. 6 cohort floor is a licensing severity band.

### Protocols

- A18-P1 (intake). An enforcement organ receives an X1-I14 occurrence, logs and attributes it to candidate actor(s), and binds it to a matter identity per K2-I32. Intake confers no coercive effect.
- A18-P2 (classification). The acting enforcement organ (a) derives offense_kind from the A5 offense_norm_set, refusing any member that fails the A18-I7 definiteness standard; (b) tests cross_frame_reach under the A18-I8 burden, recording non-aligned frame attestation; (c) grades floor_breach_severity in {none, cohort_floor, threatened_inviolable, inviolable}. The kind and the floor grade are independently re-derivable by an A6 court and are never inherited from a V4 booking.
- A18-P3 (threshold computation). The acting enforcement organ computes max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity), applying the A18-I4 fail-closed interim cap while f() is unpublished. A statistical-only basis caps the outcome at cohort-restitution or organization standing_loss (A18-I9). No hard rung is emitted without the adjudicated A6 finding required by A18-I6.
- A18-P4 (routing and referral). The organ routes by kind per A18-I2 and refers the matter to an A6 court for the adjudicated finding before any hard rung. Pre-adjudication, only a provisional_freeze is permitted. A capacity mismatch triggers the mandatory upstream tracing of A18-I11 before the matter may close.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Attempt or endangerment: an act that credibly threatens the dignity_floor with no completed effect is graded floor_breach_severity=threatened_inviolable, scaled by the confidence and cross-frame reach of the risk, and gated through A18-I4 without waiting for materialized harm.
- Misbooked floor breach: if a floor breach was booked into the priced class and settled, the settlement is void ab initio (A18-I3); the A6 court re-derives the kind and the matter reopens as an under-classification ground, not a concealment ground.
- Scapegoat with no means: attribution to a low-capacity actor does not close the occurrence; A18-I11 forces upstream tracing, and a bought or uncorroborated admission inconsistent with demonstrated capacity is disregarded.
- Laundered self-report: a partial self-report that omits a material element earns no finality; the whole matter flips perpetual, and the self-reporter carries the burden of showing disclosure was complete.
- Interim before A10: with f() unpublished, the strongest available outcome on any finding is annotation, except objective-corroborated priced-class harm which may reach priced_debit; no capability_restriction or higher issues.

### Interfaces

- A5: supplies offense definitions; A18-I7 imposes the definiteness precondition and routes vagueness challenges to A6.
- A6: adjudicates findings, re-derives kind and floor grade, hears cross_frame and frame-determination contests, and lifts finality only on a threshold-clearing concealment finding.
- A9: owns the single dignity_floor object referenced by A18-I12 and the conditions-of-restriction minima.
- A10: owns and versions f(); until it publishes, the A18-I4 fail-closed interim governs.
- K2 / X1: supply responsibility share, matter identity (K2-I32) and the victim-originated case-opener; the silence carve-out of A18-I1 governs their interaction with confidence.
- V4 / V5: V4 bookings are non-authoritative for kind; V5 frame-holders furnish non-aligned cross-frame attestation.

### External bindings

- Charter Art. 4: the inviolable rights composing the dignity_floor LIMIT (via A9).
- Charter Art. 6: cohort and minority floors, licensed as the cohort_floor severity band.
- nulla poena sine lege and lex certa / void-for-vagueness: bound by A18-I7.
- Presumption of innocence (ICCPR-analog): bound by A18-I6; the hard-rung finding precondition and the suspensive-contest rule implement it.

### Open questions

- A18-OQ1: The parameterization of the threatened_inviolable band relative to a materialized inviolable breach, and whether it may ever reach custodial_restriction pre-materialization, is deferred to A10 calibration; the fail-closed interim caps it below hard rungs meanwhile.
- A18-OQ2: The corroboration quantum for a non-aligned frame attestation (how many V5 frame-holders, and the independence test that qualifies them) is to be fixed by A6 evidentiary rules.
- A18-OQ3: How the perpetual-class flip on a material self-report omission (A18-I10) interacts with A9 rehabilitation and expiry conditions once a self-reporter later completes disclosure.
- A18-OQ4: Whether an organization-level standing_loss grounded on statistical attribution (A18-I9) requires a heavier non-aligned corroboration standard than an individualized priced-class finding.

## A18 investigation: powers, evidence and limits

### Purpose

Investigation is the single most dangerous power the polity holds: it can read, compel, freeze and restrain a person before any A6 finding exists. This section governs how an enforcement organ may open, conduct, escalate, disclose and close an investigation, and the evidentiary and rights limits binding every step. It protects both flanks at maximum force: an offender SHALL NOT escape through under-investigation, collusive closure or run-out clocks, and a person SHALL NOT be surveilled, compelled or coerced through investigation as a substitute for adjudication. Investigation itself issues no coercion rung; all coercive effect on a person remains gated by the canonical ladder and calibration owned by A18c/A18f. This section supplies only the pre-finding powers and their limits.

### Object model

- **investigation_case**: opened by an acting enforcement organ against one or more named subject_refs on a stated offense_norm_set, bound to exactly one matter.
- **matter**: per A18-I25, the underlying occurrence or aggregated-flow identity (the composed K3/K5 structure of K2-I32), not any legal characterization; relabeling the same conduct is the same matter.
- **inquiry**: any person-directed or cohort-directed information-gathering conducted before an investigation_case is opened (open-source reads, cohort queries). Inquiry is inside the log-and-attribute regime (A18-I16) and is duration- and intensity-capped per subject (A18-I13).
- **investigative_measure**: an act on the measure ladder (A18-I18), each carrying a rung, an authorization grade and a mandatory log entry.
- **preservation_freeze**: a reversible, magnitude- and duration-capped hold on assets or capabilities to prevent dissipation or continuing harm; explicitly NOT the priced_debit rung (A18-I21).
- **deferred_notice_order**: an A6-authorized suspension of a subject's contemporaneous-notice right, counted against the per-subject cumulative ceiling (A18-I22).
- **independent_advocate / special_advocate**: a funded advocate for the subject (A18-I17); the special_advocate additionally tests sealed material the subject cannot see (A18-I23, A18-I24).
- **dignity_floor**: the single canonical A9-owned inviolable set (Charter Art. 4 rights plus the A9 conditions-of-restriction minima); referenced here by name only, never re-enumerated (A18-I26).
- **acting enforcement organ**: any organ under an A12 investigative mandate; there is no apex investigator, per the no-apex polycentrism invariant (A18-I53).

### Invariants

- **A18-I13 (no fishing, no statistical individual coercion)**: Cohort statistics, market-share estimates, profiles and S5 signals MAY ground cohort-level restitution and organization-level standing_loss ONLY. They SHALL NEVER supply the confidence basis for any hard rung (capability_restriction and above) against an individual natural person, who requires individualized, non-statistical, objective-corroborated evidence. A profile or S5 signal SHALL NOT direct any inquiry or measure at a named person; person-directed inquiry requires an independent, articulable predicate logged under A18-I16, and its cumulative duration and intensity per subject are capped.
- **A18-I14 (calibration inherited, fail-closed interim)**: Investigative ceilings inherit the canonical function max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity), a monotone, capped, uniform, versioned function computed with no per-case discretion. floor_breach_severity is both a gate (must be present for any hard rung) and a multiplicand (severity scales the ceiling), and includes Charter Art. 6 cohort-floor breaches as a severity band distinct from the Art. 4 inviolable breach. The asserting organ bears the burden of proving cross_frame_reach to the same corroborated standard as confidence, attested by frames NOT aligned with the charging organ (V5 frame-holders or the affected cohort), independently contestable before any hard rung. Until A10 ratifies and publishes f(), the fail-closed interim applies: max_rung is capped at annotation except for objective-corroborated priced-class harm which may reach priced_debit, and NO hard rung issues from investigation on an unpublished or unversioned function.
- **A18-I15 (prior authorization)**: Every investigative_measure at rung 3 (search-analog) and above requires prior A6 authorization on the specific subject and scope. The sole exception is an enumerated imminent-threat exigency governed by A18e; investigation SHALL NOT self-certify exigency, and the burden of proving infeasibility of prior authorization is on the organ to the reviewing court, with a presumption of anti-value if unmet.
- **A18-I16 (log and attribute)**: Every investigative act, including pre-case inquiry, SHALL be logged, timestamped, attributed to the acting organ and officer, and bound to its stated predicate. An unlogged act is void as evidence and books anti-value against the organ.
- **A18-I17 (advocate from first coercive contact)**: Any target subject to a compelled interview, a detention-analog measure, or any measure at rung 3 (capability_restriction-analog) and above has the right to an independent, funded advocate consulted before and present during the measure. The right triggers at first coercive or custodial contact, not at charge. A measure taken in its denial is excluded (A18-I26) and books anti-value against the organ.
- **A18-I18 (measure ladder, least-intrusive)**: Investigative intrusion is ordered: (1) open-source and lawful-cohort inquiry, (2) voluntary interview, (3) compelled production or search-analog access, (4) preservation_freeze, (5) detention-analog restriction of liberty, (6) compelled biometric or bodily sampling. Each rung requires a predicate and authorization no weaker than the rung below; a higher rung SHALL NOT issue where a lower rung would suffice. No rung reaches or breaches the dignity_floor.
- **A18-I19 (self-incrimination, silence, act of production)**: No natural person SHALL be compelled to give testimonial self-incriminating content. In any criminal-class matter no adverse inference of any weight SHALL be drawn from a natural person's silence, full stop. The K2-P4 / X1-I14 victim-originated non-response inference is confined to non-criminal priced-class case-openers, is barred wherever criminal reclassification of the same act is possible, and once an occurrence is classified rights_violation the inference is inert for the A18 confidence factor: it MAY sustain the case-opener's survival but SHALL NOT contribute to confidence_grade. A good-faith, court-registered privilege claim over a testimonially-incriminating act of production is adjudicated by the court with a special_advocate and is not itself concealment; concealment attaches only to knowingly false or incomplete production, or to withholding after the claim is pierced by a narrow court finding.
- **A18-I20 (lex certa gate)**: Every A5 offense definition composing an offense_norm_set SHALL meet a definiteness standard (conduct ascertainable in advance), void-for-vagueness reviewable at A6. Investigation, classification and coercion are refused on any offense whose definition fails it.
- **A18-I21 (preservation freeze, not punishment)**: Pre-adjudication, only a reversible preservation_freeze is permitted against a person's assets or capabilities. It is a preservation measure, explicitly NOT the priced_debit rung, and is magnitude- and duration-capped. Actual forfeiture and any accruing continuous debit require an A6 attested or confirmed finding, or an uncontested attestation after a defined contest window. Contest has suspensive effect: accrual against a person freezes once contested until adjudicated, with automatic restoration-with-interest if the finding is not sustained.
- **A18-I22 (deferred notice, cumulative per subject)**: A deferred_notice_order is capped at a statutory maximum with at most one renewal. The absolute ceiling is cumulative PER SUBJECT across all related matters and re-scopings, not per order. Any new deferral touching an already-deferred subject counts against the same absolute clock and SHALL be justified to A6 as genuinely distinct.
- **A18-I23 (disclosure and gisting floor)**: On charge, and on the lifting of any deferral, the subject SHALL receive every read performed and the evidence gathered against them. A narrow sealed class MAY be disclosed only to a special_advocate, but sealed material SHALL NEVER be the sole or decisive basis for any hard rung: the subject SHALL receive a gist sufficient to give effective instructions, and where the state will not gist, the material is inadmissible for that rung. The special_advocate tests but does not substitute for the subject's own answer on decisive material.
- **A18-I24 (witness and source protection)**: Identity and identifying metadata of reporters and at-risk witnesses go only to an independent special_advocate, never to the accused, with the substance delivered as an anonymized projection sufficient to answer. Where identity is genuinely indispensable to the defense the court first imposes protective measures. Any accused-side attempt to unmask or retaliate is aggravated anti-value and forfeits reliance on the affected material.
- **A18-I25 (harassment bar, one live matter, finality, tolling)**: At most one live investigation_case exists per (subject, matter). Finality: an acquittal or settlement attaches double-jeopardy protection and bars re-charge even on new evidence, reopenable only for newly discovered concealment or fraud by that party that independently clears the hard-rung confidence threshold (high-confidence, objective, corroborated); a matter closed WITHOUT charge carries the weaker standard of genuinely new evidence plus A6 leave. Any acquittal, settlement or no-charge closure procured through a captured, colluding or conflicted charging organ or investigator is void ab initio and attaches no finality; fraud on the tribunal by EITHER party is an express reopening ground adjudicated by a court independent of the accused organ. A clean organ's investigation is never barred or consolidated away by a tainted prior proceeding; consolidation routes to the more-independent or higher-TrackRecord organ, never the earlier or lower one. Every limitation clock tolls while the offender obstructs, conceals, stalls or places assets or persons beyond reach, and starts at discovery wherever the offender's own conduct delayed it; obstruction is an independent chargeable offense that flips the underlying matter into the no-limitation class.
- **A18-I26 (dignity_floor is an absolute limit)**: The dignity_floor is a hard LIMIT outside the coercion calculus entirely. No investigative_measure SHALL reach or breach it; a measure that does is void ab initio, excluded as evidence, and books anti-value against the acting organ. Any narrower local restatement of the floor is void and does not shrink it.

### Protocols

- **A18-P5 (open an investigation)**: Validate the offense_norm_set against A18-I20; reject if any composing offense is void-for-vagueness. Individuate the matter by occurrence or aggregated-flow identity (A18-I25); route disputed individuation to the A6 resolver. Confirm no colliding live case exists per (subject, matter). Register subject_refs and the articulable predicate; open the log (A18-I16).
- **A18-P6 (escalate a measure)**: Determine the target rung (A18-I18) and confirm the predicate and least-intrusive alternative. Obtain prior A6 authorization for rung 3 and above (A18-I15). Trigger the advocate right at first coercive contact (A18-I17). For any asset or capability hold, issue only a preservation_freeze within its caps (A18-I21). For cross_frame_reach assertions, secure non-aligned attestation (A18-I14). Log the act and its attribution.
- **A18-P7 (notice, deferral, disclosure)**: Default to contemporaneous notice. To defer, obtain an A6 deferred_notice_order counted against the subject's cumulative ceiling (A18-I22). On charge or lifting, disclose all reads and evidence with a sufficient gist (A18-I23), routing witness and source identity only to the special_advocate (A18-I24).
- **A18-P8 (close or reopen)**: Before any closure, on a capacity mismatch (the attributed actor demonstrably lacks the means the harm required) upstream tracing to principals, procurers and beneficial owners is mandatory; an uncorroborated admission inconsistent with demonstrated capacity is not relied upon, and closure with one party never bars pursuit of undisclosed principals on the same occurrence. Record closure type (acquittal or settlement vs closed-without-charge) to fix the applicable reopening standard (A18-I25). A collusive or captured closure is void ab initio and reopenable without any finality bar; a below-threshold concealment claim yields at most annotation and does not reopen a settled matter.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Pre-case inquiry runs long**: On cap exhaustion (A18-I13), inquiry SHALL either escalate to an opened investigation_case with a logged predicate or cease. Profile-driven indefinite inquiry is barred, and the harassment bar reaches pre-case inquiry.
- **Wrongful pre-charge coercion**: A subject who suffered a detention-analog measure, preservation_freeze or search-analog access that does not result in a sustained charge, or is later found unfounded, is restored on every damaged axis with the state as payer, on the same no-limitation, discovery-based basis as the wrongful-penalty remedy, independent of whether any penalty_order ever issued.
- **Self-report**: Finality is strict to the fully and accurately disclosed scope; any material omission voids finality for the ENTIRE matter and flips it perpetual, with the burden on the self-reporter to show disclosure was complete.
- **Minor or incapacitated subject**: Capacity is a contestable, court-found element the charging organ must establish before any rung above annotation; a guardian ad litem is appointed in addition to the advocate; a detention-analog measure against a minor is barred save an A9-justified last resort with a hard short cap and heightened review.
- **Contradictory orders**: Contradictory coercive orders on one subject are automatically stayed pending A6 resolution, backed by the binding live-matter registry and a first-seized default a court may reassign.

### Interfaces

- **A5**: supplies offense definitions; A18-I20 gates their definiteness.
- **A6**: authorizes measures, resolves matter individuation and consolidation, is the neutral jurisdiction (structurally outside the enforcement organs) for finality and reopening, and holds the live-matter registry.
- **A9**: owns the dignity_floor referenced by A18-I26 and the conditions-of-restriction minima.
- **A18c/A18f**: own the canonical coercion ladder {annotation, priced_debit, capability_restriction, standing_loss, custodial_restriction} and calibration inherited by A18-I14; investigation issues no coercion rung itself.
- **A18e**: owns exigent force; investigation defers to it and never self-certifies exigency.
- **K2/K3/K5**: supply the occurrence and aggregated-flow identity defining a matter.

### External bindings

- Charter Art. 4 (inviolable rights) and Art. 6 (cohort floors), reached through the A9 dignity_floor and the floor_breach_severity bands of A18-I14.
- ICCPR Art. 14 fair-trial guarantees inform the advocate, disclosure, gisting and silence invariants (A18-I17, A18-I19, A18-I23).

### Open questions

1. The precise cumulative-intensity metric and per-subject duration cap for pre-case inquiry (A18-I13) await A10 calibration; the fail-closed default is the shortest bound.
2. The numeric magnitude and duration caps on a preservation_freeze (A18-I21) are set by A10 and MAY be tightened but never loosened.
3. The individuation rule distinguishing a genuinely severable multi-occurrence matter from an evasive re-scoping under the deferred-notice cumulative ceiling (A18-I22) needs an A6 test to prevent both evasion and improper joinder.

## A18c the enforcement ladder and coercion calibration

### Purpose

This section fixes the single closed ladder of coercive consequence and the single uniform function that sets its ceiling. It exists so that the strength of any measure applied to a person is a computed, versioned, reproducible output of three named factors, never per-case official discretion, and so that no measure, however calibrated, ever touches the inviolable floor. It binds both flanks: it forbids escalating pre-adjudication punishment of a disfavored person, and it forbids capping a genuine floor breach at soft power. Classification is owned by A18a, investigation by A18b, penalty assembly by A18d, use of force by A18e, charge and proof by A18f; this section owns only the rung vocabulary and the calibration gate they all invoke.

### Object model

- coercion_rung: the ordered position of a measure on the closed ladder (A18-I27).
- max_rung: the licensed ceiling for a finding, an output of the calibration function (A18-I28).
- calibration_fn f(): the A10-owned, versioned, published function over three factors; this section fixes its signature and monotonicity only.
- confidence_class: the graded strength of individualized, objective, corroborated evidence (A18-I29).
- cross_frame_reach: the graded, independently-attested objectivity of the harm across frames (A18-I30).
- floor_breach_severity: ordered {none, threatened-inviolable, cohort-floor, inviolable}, both licensing gate and multiplicand (A18-I28); distinct from the floor in its LIMIT role (A18-I36).
- escrow_freeze: a reversible, magnitude- and duration-capped preservation measure; explicitly NOT the priced_debit rung (A18-I34).
- measure / penalty_order: an issued rung with its scope, level, factor attestations, and function version id.
- reversal_order: the make-whole instrument executed by an organ independent of the issuer (A18-I38).
- dignity_floor: the single A9-owned canonical floor object, referenced by name, never re-enumerated here (A18-I36).

### Invariants

- A18-I27 (closed ordered ladder): the coercion ladder is one closed ascending set of rungs, {annotation, priced_debit, capability_restriction, standing_loss, custodial_restriction}. Prosecution is a distinct procedural step preceding custodial_restriction, named explicitly and never folded into the custodial rung. No rung outside this set exists; every other section, including A18f coercion_rung, SHALL use this vocabulary verbatim.
- A18-I28 (calibration function): max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity), a monotone, capped, uniform, versioned, published function computed with NO per-case discretion. floor_breach_severity is BOTH a gate (a qualifying breach value must be present for any hard rung) AND a multiplicand (its severity scales the ceiling). Its value cohort-floor covers Charter Art. 6 minority and cohort-floor breaches and licenses the hard ladder; threatened-inviolable grades attempt and endangerment on the confidence and cross-frame reach of the RISK, so endangerment gates without materialized harm. FAIL-CLOSED INTERIM: until A10 ratifies and publishes a versioned f(), max_rung is capped at annotation for all findings, except objective-corroborated priced-class harm which MAY reach priced_debit; NO hard rung (capability_restriction and above) SHALL issue on an unpublished or unversioned function.
- A18-I29 (confidence discipline; no statistical hard coercion of individuals): confidence_class SHALL be multi-source, staked, calibration-bound and revaluable. Purely statistical or market-share attribution MAY ground cohort-level restitution and organization-level standing_loss ONLY, and SHALL NEVER supply the confidence basis for any hard rung against an individual natural person, who requires individualized, non-statistical, objective-corroborated evidence. The proof standard rises with the rung: clear-and-corroborated for capability_restriction and standing_loss; the highest standard, near-certainty or beyond reasonable doubt, for custodial_restriction and any criminal-class rights_violation finding (A18f). In any criminal-class matter a natural person's silence contributes ZERO weight to the confidence factor (A18f, A18b).
- A18-I30 (cross-frame anti-gaming): cross_frame_reach carries the same evidentiary discipline as confidence. The burden of proving cross-frame objectivity is on the asserting organ to the corroborated standard; the objectivity SHALL be attested by frames NOT aligned with the charging organ, that is V5 frame-holders or the affected cohort; and the frame determination is independently contestable before any hard rung. An intra-frame preference relabeled as cross-frame harm fails the gate.
- A18-I31 (least-restrictive and proportionality): every measure SHALL be the least-restrictive means achieving the restorative or preventive objective and proportionate to floor_breach_severity and reach. Coercion exceeding the least-restrictive or proportionality finding is over-coercion and books anti-value against the acting enforcement organ (A18e); it is not cured by any good-faith reliance (A18e).
- A18-I32 (soft/hard line; presumption of innocence): No HARD rung (capability_restriction and above) SHALL apply to a person except on an adjudicated A6 finding. Soft rungs (annotation, priced_debit) sense-with-consequence but remain provisional, contestable and reversible; a provisional offense booking confers no coercive effect of itself. priced_debit forfeiture and any continuous debit are NOT automatic punishment on a person (A18-I34).
- A18-I33 (kind routing): the acting enforcement organ routes by offense_kind: rights_violation to the criminal in-kind ladder (non-priceable); priced_anti_value to the priced class; systemic_harm to priced-class cohort restitution, plus the criminal ladder only where it also breaches a Charter Art. 4 right. A Charter Art. 6 cohort-floor systemic breach routes to cohort restitution and, via floor_breach_severity=cohort-floor, licenses the hard ladder.
- A18-I34 (provisional freeze, not forfeiture): pre-adjudication, only a reversible provisional escrow_freeze is permitted, defined as a preservation measure and explicitly NOT the priced_debit rung, and magnitude- and duration-capped. Actual forfeiture and any accruing continuous debit require an A6 attested or confirmed finding, or an uncontested attestation after a defined contest window. Contest has SUSPENSIVE effect: accrual against a person freezes once contested until adjudicated, with automatic restoration-with-interest if the finding is not sustained.
- A18-I35 (bounded capability suspension; no transitive collective punishment): capability_restriction reaches only capabilities the offender still controls or whose continued exercise perpetuates the measured harm, and walks the S1/S2 delegation graph ONLY along harm-channeling links. Good-faith downstream delegatees receive individual notice, standing to contest, an innocent-holder carve-out, and a substitution or re-grant path preserving their participation floor (Charter Art. 12), with restoration if unattributable. A downstream holder with no adjudicated responsibility share SHALL NEVER be restricted as punishment.
- A18-I36 (floor is a LIMIT, not a factor): the inviolable floor is the single A9-owned canonical dignity_floor object; this section references it by name and SHALL NOT re-enumerate it. It is a hard LIMIT outside the coercion calculus entirely: no rung, however calibrated, may breach it, and no measure is ever inside the lawful set if it intrudes on bodily integrity. Any narrower local restatement is void and does not shrink the floor.
- A18-I37 (anti-revenue): any commons fund receiving deterrent, concentration-charge or clawback proceeds SHALL be governed independently of the entire enforcement and state-budget chain; no organ in the enforcing mandate lineage benefits from or directs it. Restoration-first exhaustion precedes any deterrent routing, and the routing is fully traced and publicly auditable.
- A18-I38 (reversibility and irreversibility ex-ante): a reversal_order restores the coerced party by maximal feasible restitution plus compensation on irreversible axes. Because reversal cannot make whole, custodial_restriction, liberty restriction and any physically non-restorable capability loss require the TOP confidence band ex ante; this stringency is a pre-condition of issue, not an impossible ex-post promise of undoing.

### Protocols

- A18-P9 (ceiling calibration): given a classified finding, the acting enforcement organ computes max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity) using the published versioned function; where f() is unpublished or unversioned, the A18-I28 interim cap applies. The cross-frame determination (A18-I30) and any statistical-attribution flag (A18-I29) SHALL be resolved and independently contestable before the ceiling licenses any hard rung. Output: max_rung, function version id, and per-factor attestations.
- A18-P10 (soft path, freeze then finalize): on priced-class measurement the acting enforcement organ MAY place a reversible provisional escrow_freeze (A18-I34), capped in magnitude and duration and booked as a preservation measure, not a rung. Notice issues to the subject with the contest window. On contest, finalization and any accrual are suspended pending A6 (suspensive effect). Forfeiture or continuous debit book only on an A6 attested or confirmed finding, or an uncontested attestation after the window; if the finding is not sustained, the freeze is released and the subject restored with interest.
- A18-P11 (hard-rung issuance): no rung at capability_restriction or above issues except on an adjudicated A6 finding (A18-I32), within max_rung (A18-P9), at the least-restrictive proportionate level (A18-I31), and never in breach of the dignity_floor (A18-I36). capability_restriction walks only harm-channeling delegation links (A18-I35), and each downstream holder caught receives notice, contest, and a re-grant path. custodial_restriction additionally requires the top confidence band (A18-I38) and the highest proof standard (A18-I29).
- A18-P12 (reversal and restoration): on a sustained reversal ground, an organ independent of the issuer executes a reversal_order, delivering maximal feasible restitution plus compensation on irreversible axes, restoration-with-interest of any frozen or debited value, and unwinding of delegation-graph effects on innocent holders. Where proportionality or floor limits were exceeded, the reversal books anti-value against the over-coercing organ.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Uncalibrated function: before A10 publishes a versioned f(), every finding is annotation-only, except objective-corroborated priced-class harm which may reach priced_debit; no hard rung issues.
- Attempt or endangerment: floor_breach_severity is set to threatened-inviolable, graded on the confidence and cross-frame reach of the risk, licensing proportionate coercion without materialized harm.
- Contested priced debit: the freeze holds but accrual and finalization suspend until A6 adjudicates; a non-sustained finding restores with interest.
- Innocent downstream delegatee: a holder with no adjudicated responsibility share is never restricted as punishment; they receive notice, contest, and substitution preserving their participation floor.
- Irreversible measure: custodial or physically non-restorable capability loss issues only at the top confidence band, ex ante.
- Cohort-floor systemic breach: a Charter Art. 6 breach licenses the hard ladder via the cohort-floor severity band, so minority-protection breaches are not capped at soft power.
- Over-coercion within authority class: exceeding the proportionality or least-restrictive finding is anti-value against the organ and is not excused by staying inside the officer's class (A18e).

### Interfaces

- A6: adjudicates the finding that any hard rung requires, hears contests with suspensive effect, and acts as the neutral consolidation and conflict resolver for contradictory orders.
- A9: owns the canonical dignity_floor object and the conditions-of-restriction minima referenced by A18-I36.
- A10: ratifies, versions and publishes f(); may tighten the interim cap and the caps of A18-I34 but never loosen the floor or the gate.
- A5: supplies offense definitions; coercion is refused on any offense whose definition fails the A6 definiteness review it imports from A18a.
- A12: sets the plural organ mandates; no apex enforcer exists, so "the acting enforcement organ" is always organ-neutral and plural-capable.
- S1/S2: the delegation graph walked only along harm-channeling links.

### External bindings

- S7 Value-Money-Coupling: priced_debit and continuous debit book here, but only as provisional and reversible until finalized per A18-I34.
- K2: responsibility share governs who may be placed on any rung and bounds the delegation walk.
- A18a classification and kind; A18b investigation and advocate rights; A18d penalty assembly and clawback; A18e use of force and good-faith limits; A18f charge, proof standards and finality.
- Charter Art. 4 (inviolable floor as LIMIT), Art. 6 (cohort floor as licensing band), Art. 12 (participation floor for innocent holders).

### Open questions

1. The numeric caps on provisional escrow_freeze magnitude and duration, and the length of the contest window, await A9 and A10 parameterization; the interim rule holds until then.
2. The precise curve of f(), its weights and caps, is owned by A10; this section fixes only its three-factor signature, monotonicity and interim cap.
3. The threshold test for classifying a delegation link as harm-channeling where a downstream holder's exercise is partly independent of the offender.
4. The interest basis and make-whole computation for restoration-with-interest on suspended accrual and released freezes.

## A18 penalty, correction and restoration

### Purpose

A18d assembles the consequence from an adjudicated A6 finding, executes correction and restoration, and governs reopening and limitation at the penalty stage. Its governing priority is restoration-first: the measured harm to the affected set SHALL be made good before any deterrent, concentration-charge or clawback proceeds are routed anywhere. It draws consequences only from the one canonical ladder, treats the A9-owned inviolable_floor as an absolute limit outside the coercion calculus, and provides the symmetric remedy when the polity itself coerces wrongly. No apex penalizer exists: any acting enforcement organ that executes a penalty does so under a distinct A12 mandate and under A6 supervision.

### Object model

- penalty_order: an ordered subset of the canonical ladder rungs, each rung anchored to a specific A6 finding, with its ceiling fixed by the A18-I52 calibration function. The canonical rung vocabulary is the closed ordered set {annotation, priced_debit, capability_restriction, standing_loss, custodial_restriction}; prosecution, where it is a distinct step, is named explicitly and is never folded into custodial_restriction.
- restitution_computation: the reproducible mapping from affected_set to restoration on each damaged axis. Objective axes are restored to measured harm; bounded-subjective axes follow published schedules. The computation is publicly reproducible and separately appealable.
- affected_set: the measured cohort of harmed parties (identified victims plus the S5 aggregate), contestable in scope and axis classification by the cohort_advocate.
- clawback_claim: a value-recovery claim that follows the S1/S2 graph to the natural-person beneficial owner, carrying the criminal-class in-kind consequence personally where the kind is rights_violation.
- escrow_bond: risk-proportionate pre-funding posted at the point of undertaking high-harm-capacity activity, so restoration is pre-funded rather than backstopped by the commons.
- provisional_freeze: a reversible, magnitude- and duration-capped preservation measure. It is explicitly NOT the priced_debit rung and confers no adjudicated consequence.
- limitation_clock: the per-matter clock; tolled during obstruction and set to the perpetual (no-limitation) class for floor breaches, obstruction, and materially incomplete self-reports.
- restoration_order: restores a wrongly coerced person on every feasible axis plus compensation on irreversible axes, with the state as payer.
- commons_routing_record: the fully traced, publicly auditable record of any surplus routed to a commons fund after restoration is exhausted.
- matter: the underlying occurrence or aggregated-flow identity (the composed K3/K5 structure of K2-I32), never the legal characterization; relabeling the same conduct is the same matter.

### Invariants

A18-I41 (restoration primacy and cohort standing): Restoration of the measured affected_set SHALL precede and outrank any deterrent, concentration-charge or clawback routing. An independent cohort_advocate SHALL have standing to contest affected_set scope, axis classification and restoration adequacy before any settlement or penalty_order closes. A settlement that under-restores the measured affected_set is void and reopenable and attaches no finality or double-jeopardy bar.

A18-I42 (reproducible restitution): The restitution_computation SHALL be publicly reproducible and separately appealable. Bounded-subjective schedules MAY cap subjective axes but SHALL NOT reduce restoration of any objective axis below measured harm. The cohort share SHALL NOT be discounted for the silence or non-participation of unidentified affected parties.

A18-I43 (anti-revenue): Restoration-first exhaustion SHALL precede any deterrent routing. Any commons fund receiving deterrent, concentration-charge or clawback proceeds SHALL be governed independently of the entire enforcement and state-budget chain; no organ in the enforcing mandate lineage SHALL benefit from, direct, or receive proceeds from it. All routing is recorded in commons_routing_record, fully traced and publicly auditable. Deterrence that funds any organ in its own enforcement lineage, directly or one organ removed, is void.

A18-I44 (kind routing): The three A18-I2 kinds map to enforcement classes as follows. rights_violation routes to the criminal class (in-kind, non-priceable). priced_anti_value routes to the priced class. systemic_harm routes to priced-class cohort restitution, plus the criminal ladder only where the same conduct also breaches a Charter Art. 4 right. A Charter Art. 6 cohort-floor systemic breach routes to priced-class cohort restitution and, as a licensing floor_breach band (A18-I52), MAY license the hard ladder against the responsible actor or organization; the Art. 4 in-kind criminal class attaches only on an Art. 4 breach. priced_debit forfeiture and any accruing continuous debit are NOT automatic punishment on a person: they require an A6 attested or confirmed finding, or an uncontested attestation after the defined contest window. Pre-adjudication only a provisional_freeze is permitted.

A18-I45 (beneficial-owner liability and pre-funding): The natural-person beneficial owner SHALL carry the criminal-class in-kind consequence personally; the S1 graph is pierced for the criminal class, not only for economic clawback. Transfers of value or harm capacity out of the conforming S1 graph after harm capacity attached are presumptive fraudulent conveyance, with the burden on the transferor and personal standing_loss on proof. Undertaking high-harm-capacity activity SHALL require a risk-proportionate escrow_bond so restoration is pre-funded, not externalized to the commons.

A18-I46 (penalty assembly bounded by the floor as limit): A penalty_order SHALL draw only from the canonical rung vocabulary. The A9-owned inviolable_floor is an absolute limit outside the coercion calculus entirely; no rung, and no execution of any rung, SHALL intrude on it, and no narrower local restatement shrinks it. No HARD rung (capability_restriction and above) SHALL apply to a person except on an adjudicated A6 finding. custodial_restriction SHALL satisfy the A9 conditions-of-restriction and remains subject to standing review of condition; its detention ceilings and review cadence are fixed in A18f and A10 may tighten but never loosen them.

A18-I47 (capacity-mismatch tracing): Where the attributed actor demonstrably lacks the means or capability the harm required, upstream tracing to principals, procurers and beneficial owners is mandatory before any closure. Conviction or settlement with one party never bars pursuit of undisclosed principals or procurers on the same occurrence. An uncorroborated admission inconsistent with the attributed actor's demonstrated capacity SHALL NOT be relied upon.

A18-I48 (no transitive collective punishment): A capability_restriction transitive walk of the S1/S2 delegation graph is limited to capabilities the offender still controls or whose continued exercise perpetuates the measured harm. A good-faith downstream delegatee SHALL receive individual notice, standing to contest, an innocent-holder carve-out, and a substitution or re-grant path preserving the Charter Art. 12 participation floor, with restoration if the linkage is unattributable. A downstream holder with no adjudicated responsibility share is never restricted as punishment. No consequence SHALL attach to any person by status or association.

A18-I49 (limitation tolling and self-report): Every limitation_clock SHALL toll for the entire interval the offender obstructs, conceals, stalls, or places assets or persons beyond reach, and SHALL start at discovery wherever the offender's own conduct delayed discovery. Obstruction of an investigation is an independent chargeable offense that flips the underlying matter into the perpetual, no-limitation class. Self-report finality is strict to the fully and accurately disclosed scope: any material omission voids finality for the entire matter and flips it perpetual, with the burden on the self-reporter to show disclosure was complete. A concealment finding SHALL independently clear the hard-rung threshold (high-confidence, objective, corroborated) before any limitation or finality bar lifts; a below-threshold concealment claim yields at most annotation and does not reopen a settled matter. Once an occurrence is classified rights_violation, the X1-I14 victim-originated non-response inference is inert for the coercion confidence factor.

A18-I50 (no floor-breach buy-out): A priced settlement purporting to discharge a Charter Art. 4 floor breach is void ab initio, with no finality or jeopardy bar, regardless of concealment, because the criminal class was never validly adjudicated. Under-classification of an on-record floor breach is an independent, non-concealment reopening ground. The kind determination SHALL be re-derived independently by the A6 court, never inherited from an offender-influenced V4 booking. A closure procured through a captured, colluding or conflicted charging organ or investigator is void ab initio and attaches no finality; a clean organ's matter is never barred or consolidated away by a tainted prior proceeding.

A18-I51 (wrongful-coercion remedy): A person subjected to any wrongful coercion SHALL be restored on every damaged axis, with the state as payer, on a no-limitation, discovery-based basis, independent of whether a penalty_order ever issued. This right extends to any coercive investigative measure (detention-analog, frozen escrow or search-analog) that does not result in a sustained charge or is later found unfounded, and to any provisional_freeze contested and not sustained, which is restored with interest. Restoration requires maximal feasible restitution plus compensation on irreversible axes; where an axis is physically non-restorable, that irreversibility is a reason for ex-ante stringency at A18-I52, not an ex-post promise the order cannot keep.

A18-I52 (calibration and the licensing gate): The ceiling on any penalty_order is max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity): a single, monotone, capped, uniform, versioned function with NO per-case discretion, published by A10. floor_breach is both a gate (it SHALL be present for any hard rung) and a multiplicand (its severity scales the ceiling); its severity bands are threatened-inviolable, cohort-floor (Charter Art. 6), and inviolable (Charter Art. 4), the last being separately the absolute limit of A18-I46. Purely statistical or market-share attribution MAY ground cohort-level restitution and organization-level standing_loss ONLY, and SHALL NEVER supply the confidence basis for any hard rung against an individual natural person, who requires individualized, non-statistical, objective-corroborated evidence. FAIL-CLOSED INTERIM: until A10 ratifies and publishes f(), max_rung is capped at annotation for all findings except objective-corroborated priced-class harm, which may reach priced_debit; NO hard rung issues on an unpublished or unversioned function.

### Protocols

A18-P13 (penalty assembly): Given an A6 finding, an acting enforcement organ SHALL (1) confirm the offense definition met the A5 lex-certa standard and was not refused as void-for-vagueness; (2) take the kind re-derived by the court (A18-I50) and route it by A18-I44; (3) compute max_rung by A18-I52, honoring the fail-closed interim if f() is unpublished; (4) assemble the penalty_order from the canonical ladder at or below max_rung, applying the least-restrictive measure; (5) verify no rung nor its execution touches the A9 inviolable_floor. A hard rung absent an adjudicated finding is refused.

A18-P14 (restoration and clawback): The acting enforcement organ SHALL (1) publish the restitution_computation and notify the cohort_advocate, who may contest before closure; (2) restore the affected_set on all objective axes to measured harm and on bounded-subjective axes per schedule; (3) recover value by clawback_claim along the S1 graph, piercing to the natural-person beneficial owner for the criminal class and pursuing presumptive fraudulent conveyances; (4) draw first on any escrow_bond; (5) route surplus only after restoration is exhausted, and only to an independently governed commons fund recorded in commons_routing_record. Under-restoration voids the closure (A18-I41).

A18-P15 (wrongful-coercion remedy): Where a penalty is reversed, or a coercive investigative measure or provisional_freeze does not result in a sustained charge or is found unfounded, an organ independent of the one that coerced SHALL open a restoration_order, restore the wronged person on every feasible axis with compensation on irreversible axes, restore any frozen value with interest, and book the shortfall to the state as payer on the no-limitation, discovery-based basis of A18-I51.

A18-P16 (reopening, limitation and finality at penalty): On any claim to reopen, the A6 court SHALL (1) individuate the matter by occurrence identity (K2-I32), routing disputed individuation to the A6 resolver; (2) apply tolling and the perpetual-class flips of A18-I49; (3) treat a floor-breach buy-out, under-classification, capacity-mismatch or collusive closure as void per A18-I50; (4) require a concealment finding to clear the hard-rung threshold before lifting any bar; (5) on capacity mismatch, require upstream tracing before closure (A18-I47). Finality and double-jeopardy attach on acquittal or settlement (concealment or fraud reopening only, per A18f); a matter closed without charge carries the weaker new-evidence-plus-leave standard.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Priced-class same-tick sensing: a measured priced harm may drive a provisional_freeze immediately as a preservation measure. It is not the priced_debit rung and confers no adjudicated consequence. Contest has suspensive effect: accrual against a person freezes once contested until adjudicated, and if the finding is not sustained the freeze is released and restored with interest (A18-P15).
- Systemic harm without an Art. 4 breach: routes to cohort restitution and, where a Charter Art. 6 cohort floor is breached, may reach the hard ladder against the responsible organization or actor; the in-kind criminal class does not attach absent an Art. 4 breach.
- Shell dissolution: the criminal class does not dissolve with the entity. The natural-person beneficial owner carries it personally (A18-I45); an empty economic claim is never accepted as discharge.
- Scapegoat closure: an attribution to a low-capacity actor never closes the occurrence; capacity mismatch mandates upstream tracing before any closure (A18-I47).
- Laundered self-report: a self-report that omits a floor breach banks no finality; the omission flips the entire matter perpetual, burden on the self-reporter (A18-I49).
- Irreversible penalty: custodial_restriction and any physically non-restorable capability loss require the top confidence band ex ante (A18-I52) precisely because reversal cannot make whole.
- Cohort under-restoration: a settlement satisfying vocal complainants while discounting the S5 aggregate is void and reopenable on the cohort_advocate's motion (A18-I41).

### Interfaces

- A6: adjudicates the finding, re-derives the kind, appoints the cohort_advocate, resolves matter individuation and consolidation, and adjudicates all reopening.
- A9: owns the inviolable_floor referenced by A18-I46 and the conditions-of-restriction governing custodial_restriction.
- A10: ratifies, versions and publishes the calibration function f(); the fail-closed interim binds until it does.
- A5: supplies offense definitions, which must meet the lex-certa standard enforced at A18a and A6.
- A12: scopes the mandate under which each acting enforcement organ executes a penalty_order.
- S1/S2: the delegation and ownership graph walked for clawback (A18-P14) and bounded for capability_restriction (A18-I48).
- S5: supplies the aggregate harm measure defining the affected_set.
- V4: books the provisional kind, which the court re-derives rather than inherits (A18-I50).
- K2/K3/K5, X1: define matter and occurrence identity and the case-opener whose non-response inference is inert once criminal.

### External bindings

- Charter Art. 4 inviolable rights define the criminal class and the absolute limit of A18-I46.
- Charter Art. 6 cohort floors define the cohort-floor severity band that licenses the hard ladder (A18-I52) without themselves being the Art. 4 limit.
- Charter Art. 12 participation floor bounds the transitive capability walk and the innocent-holder carve-out (A18-I48).
- Fair-trial and non-retroactivity norms bind the reopening standards to concealment or fraud only for an honest acquittal (A18-P16, A18f).

### Open questions

1. The precise magnitude and duration caps on a provisional_freeze await A9 and A10; until set, the shortest defensible cap applies.
2. The published form and versioning cadence of f() await A10 ratification; the fail-closed interim of A18-I52 governs in the meantime.
3. The escrow_bond schedule per harm-capacity class awaits A9 and A10 calibration.
4. The registry of non-aligned frames competent to attest cross-frame objectivity (A18a) is not yet fixed; until it is, the asserting organ carries the full corroboration burden.

## A18e enforcement agents, use of force and their accountability

### Purpose

This section governs the organs that carry out coercion in the field, the licensing and limits of the use of force, and the personal accountability of the officers who wield it. It is the sharpest rights surface of A18: both offender impunity and state over-reach are catastrophic, so the section is written to fence both flanks at once. Force here is never a standing power held by any organ; it is a per-act license computed by the shared calibration function, bounded absolutely by the canonical inviolable floor, and answerable through symmetric accountability for both action and inaction. This section owns the polycentric enforcement structure, the use-of-force limit, the exigent and emergency-force regime, the neutral conflict resolver, and the officer-accountability rules (rejection of qualified immunity, the narrowed good-faith defense, the no-superior-orders rule, and the treatment of dereliction).

### Object model

- **enforcement_organ**: a coercion-executing organ operating under a distinct A12 mandate. Always referenced organ-neutrally and in the plural; there is no definite singular enforcement agent and no apex organ.
- **coercion_license**: the per-act ceiling and selected rung produced by the calibration function for a specific measure against a specific subject; expires with the act.
- **use_of_force_act**: a logged, attributable coercive measure carrying its license, its authorization or ratification record, and a floor-non-breach attestation.
- **dignity_floor**: the single canonical A9-owned inviolable set. This section references it by name only and never re-enumerates it. It is the union of Charter Art. 4 rights and the A9 conditions-of-restriction minima: dignity, life, bodily integrity, liberty, liberty of conscience, consent, no cruel or inhuman or degrading treatment, no coerced self-incrimination, subsistence, contact, right of exit, and standing review of condition.
- **rung ladder**: the closed ordered vocabulary owned canonically as {annotation, priced_debit, capability_restriction, standing_loss, custodial_restriction}; prosecution, where it is a distinct step, is named explicitly and is never folded into the custodial label.
- **exigency_record**: an X3 incident registering an enumerated imminent threat, registered by an organ independent of the invoker.
- **force_template**: a pre-declared, A6-pre-approved pattern scoped to a narrowly specified threat type.
- **accountability_record**: the per-act log binding a coercive act, or a declination to act, to a named officer and organ.

### Invariants

- **A18-I53 (polycentric enforcement, no apex)**: Enforcement SHALL be distributed across plural enforcement organs under distinct A12 mandates, mutually anchoring, with no apex organ. No definite singular enforcement agent exists; all rules bind the acting enforcement organ.

- **A18-I54 (force is a licensed per-act capability, not a standing power)**: No enforcement organ SHALL hold standing coercive power. Each coercive act draws on a coercion_license computed by A18-I56 for that act and subject; any force outside its license books anti-value against the organ and the officer.

- **A18-I55 (prior authorization; enumerated exigency only)**: Every coercive measure SHALL carry prior A6 authorization, EXCEPT under an enumerated imminent-threat exigency registered per A18-I60. Prior authorization is mandatory unless a registered X3 exigency exists; the burden is on the organ to prove to the reviewing court that prior authorization was infeasible, with a presumption of anti-value if that burden is unmet. Any unauthorized coercive act SHALL obtain judicial ratification within a fixed short window or lapse and book anti-value; skipping authorization absent a registered exigency is per se unauthorized regardless of claimed good faith.

- **A18-I56 (canonical calibration signature; fail-closed interim)**: The ceiling on force SHALL be max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity), a monotone, capped, uniform, versioned function computed with no per-case discretion. floor_breach_severity is BOTH a gate (it must be present for any hard rung) AND a multiplicand (its severity scales the ceiling); it is stated with all three factors and never reduced to two. The confidence_class factor against an individual natural person SHALL rest on individualized, non-statistical, objective-corroborated evidence: purely statistical or market-share attribution SHALL NEVER supply the confidence basis for any hard rung against an individual, and grounds only cohort restitution and organization-level standing loss. FAIL-CLOSED INTERIM: until A10 ratifies and publishes f(), max_rung is capped at annotation for all findings except objective-corroborated priced-class harm, which may reach priced_debit; NO hard rung (capability_restriction and above) SHALL issue on an unpublished or unversioned function.

- **A18-I57 (inviolable floor as an absolute limit on force)**: No use of force SHALL breach the A9 dignity_floor. The floor is a hard LIMIT that sits outside the coercion calculus entirely: no bodily-integrity intrusion, and no breach of any floor element, is ever inside the lawful set. There is no minimized-lawful-measure exception. Any narrower local restatement of the floor is void and does not shrink it. An enforcement organ SHALL register a floor breach for any act touching a floor element, expressly including prolonged incommunicado isolation (contact and dignity), denial of subsistence, or coerced self-incrimination.

- **A18-I58 (no qualified immunity; narrowed good-faith defense)**: Qualified immunity is rejected. A good-faith factual-error defense is available to an officer ONLY where the measure was within both the coercion_authority_class AND the least-restrictive and proportionality ceiling of A18-I59, breached no floor, and the officer had no conflict and received no benefit. The defense SHALL be barred for any coercion exceeding the least-restrictive or proportionality finding, where the officer had a conflict of interest, where the officer received any benefit, or where reliance was objectively unreasonable. It is categorically unavailable for failure to act or under-enforcement that shielded an offender; such dereliction is aggravated, not excused. Reliance on a superior's order never survives (A18-I61).

- **A18-I59 (least-restrictive and proportionality; over-coercion aggravated)**: Every coercive measure SHALL be the least-restrictive effective means and proportionate to floor_breach_severity and confidence. Over-coercion, any force exceeding the least-restrictive or proportionality finding, is aggravated anti-value against the acting organ and officer even where it stays within the authority class.

- **A18-I60 (emergency-force quota with surge, never a structural bar)**: Exigent-force invocations are quota-bound per incident and per window. The triggering exigency_record SHALL be registered by an organ independent of the invoker. force_templates SHALL be capped to narrowly specified threat types with A6 pre-approval; no organ manufactures its own exigency under a broad template. On quota exhaustion the authorization requirement SHALL escalate rather than hard-deny: the dual-control grade rises and each further invocation requires immediate synchronous court ratification, so a genuine life-saving emergency is never structurally barred, only more tightly witnessed. A guaranteed independent registration capacity per incident load SHALL be maintained.

- **A18-I61 (superior orders no defense; personal duty to refuse)**: An unlawful order SHALL confer no defense. Each officer bears a personal, non-delegable duty to refuse any floor-breaching or out-of-license order; obeying such an order is personal anti-value against that officer and does not shelter the ordering organ.

- **A18-I62 (advocate at coercive contact, enforcement duty)**: An enforcement organ SHALL NOT conduct a compelled interview, a detention-analog measure, or any measure at capability_restriction (rung 3) and above without the target's independent funded advocate present and consulted before and during the measure (advocate right owned in A18b). Measures taken in its denial are excluded and book anti-value against the organ.

- **A18-I63 (neutral conflict resolver; no-apex operability)**: Because no apex exists among enforcers, A6 courts, being structurally outside the enforcement organs, are the neutral jurisdiction and consolidation resolver, backed by a binding live-matter registry and a first-seized (lis pendens) default that a court may reassign. Contradictory coercive orders on one subject are automatically stayed pending the court's resolution, and consolidation routes to the more-independent or higher-TrackRecord organ, never the earlier or lower one.

- **A18-I64 (symmetric accountability; dereliction is aggravated)**: Every coercive act AND every declination to act SHALL be logged and attributed to a named officer and organ. Under-enforcement that shielded an offender is aggravated anti-value with no good-faith or superior-order shelter (A18-I58, A18-I61). Endangerment or attempt force is gated on the threatened-inviolable floor_breach value defined in A18a, so risk may be met without waiting for materialized harm.

### Protocols

- **A18-P17 (authorized coercive measure)**: (1) An enforcement organ computes the coercion_license via A18-I56 and confirms f() is published and versioned, else the interim cap applies. (2) It obtains prior A6 authorization and verifies non-breach of the A9 dignity_floor, refusing the act on any offense failing lex certa (A5). (3) It selects the least-restrictive rung within the license (A18-I59). (4) For any measure at capability_restriction and above it seats the funded advocate (A18-I62). (5) It executes with minimal force, logs the act to the live-matter registry, and binds it to a named officer in an accountability_record.

- **A18-P18 (exigent force)**: (1) On an enumerated imminent threat the organ acts with the minimal force that respects the dignity_floor absolutely. (2) An independent organ registers the exigency_record (X3) under a narrow A6-pre-approved template. (3) The organ carries the burden of proving infeasibility of prior authorization to the reviewing court. (4) It obtains judicial ratification within the fixed window or the act lapses and books anti-value. (5) On quota exhaustion it follows the escalation path of A18-I60 rather than being denied.

- **A18-P19 (accountability review of a coercive act)**: (1) A6 reviews each contested act against its license, floor-non-breach attestation, and proportionality finding. (2) The good-faith defense is adjudicated strictly per A18-I58; over-coercion, conflict, benefit, unreasonable reliance, and dereliction defeat it. (3) Confirmed over-coercion, floor breach, dereliction, or unratified exigent force books anti-value against the officer and organ and triggers restoration to the subject. (4) Jurisdictional or double-coverage disputes route to the A18-I63 resolver, which stays contradictory orders pending resolution.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Good-faith error**: A genuinely reasonable factual error, where the measure stayed within class and the proportionality ceiling, breached no floor, and involved no conflict or benefit, yields restitution and correction with no anti-value component. Every other case, including over-coercion within class, is aggravated anti-value (A18-I58, A18-I59).
- **Over-coercion within authority class**: Excess beyond the least-restrictive or proportionality finding is aggravated anti-value; the good-faith defense is barred and cannot convert it to costless error.
- **Dereliction and under-enforcement**: Tipping off, looking away, or under-enforcing for a favored offender is categorically undefended; no superior-order or good-faith shelter applies (A18-I61, A18-I64).
- **Mass-casualty surge**: When the exigent quota is exhausted amid many genuine simultaneous emergencies, force is never hard-denied where the floor is untouched; the dual-control grade rises and synchronous court ratification is required per further invocation (A18-I60).
- **Manufactured exigency**: An organ registering its own X3 incident, or acting under a broad template, fails A18-I60; the act is per se unauthorized and books anti-value.
- **Contradictory orders and forum-shopping**: Competing coercive orders on one subject are automatically stayed and sent to the A6 resolver, which consolidates to the more-independent or higher-TrackRecord organ (A18-I63).
- **Attempt and endangerment**: Force against a credible threat with no completed effect is gated on the threatened-inviolable floor_breach value (A18a), graded on the confidence and cross-frame reach of the risk.
- **Wrongful pre-charge coercion**: A subject coerced by force who is never charged or is later found unfounded is restored on every damaged axis with the state as payer, on the discovery-based, no-limitation basis of the wrongful-coercion remedy (owned in A18f), independent of any penalty_order.

### Interfaces

- **A6**: authorizes measures, ratifies exigent acts within the fixed window, reviews accountability, and serves as the neutral consolidation resolver and lis-pendens registry (A18-I55, A18-I60, A18-I63, A18-P19).
- **A9**: owns the dignity_floor referenced by A18-I57 and the conditions-of-restriction minima.
- **A10**: ratifies and publishes the versioned calibration function f() and the detention and review ceilings; until then the fail-closed interim of A18-I56 governs.
- **A12**: issues the distinct mandates under which plural enforcement organs operate (A18-I53).
- **A5**: supplies offense definitions, which must meet lex certa or coercion is refused (A18-P17).
- **X3**: registers exigency incidents through an organ independent of the invoker (A18-I60, A18-P18).
- **S1/S2, K2**: supply the capability graph and attribution used to scope and attribute a coercive act.

### External bindings

- **Charter Art. 4**: the inviolable rights composing the jus cogens core of the dignity_floor.
- **Charter Art. 6**: cohort floors, which enter the licensing gate as a severity band distinct from the Art. 4 absolute limit.
- **Charter Art. 12**: the livelihood floor, protecting downstream holders from collective punishment by force.
- **ICCPR fair-trial analogs and customary jus cogens**: the non-derogable limits on the use of force and on custodial coercion.

### Open questions

1. The numeric per-incident and per-window exigent-force quota, and the exact length of the judicial-ratification window, are deferred to A10; the interim rule is the shortest safe value, which A10 may tighten but never loosen.
2. The precise dual-control-grade escalation schedule on quota exhaustion (how many synchronous ratifications and at what grade) awaits A10 calibration against real surge load.
3. The evidentiary boundary of "objectively unreasonable reliance" for the narrowed good-faith defense will be sharpened by accumulated A6 case law; the interim standard resolves ambiguity against the officer.
4. The guaranteed independent-registrar capacity needed so that mass-casualty surges never exhaust registration itself (as distinct from invocation quota) is an operational parameter left to A10.

## A18 due process, rights and appeals

### Purpose

This section is the accused's flank of A18. It fixes the guarantees that bind every enforcement organ before, during, and after coercion: presumption of innocence, the soft/hard line, the right to counsel and to answer the case, the proof standard for each rung, the ceilings on liberty restriction, the remedy for wrongful coercion, and the finality that protects the honest while never sheltering the colluding. It protects both flanks at once: an offender SHALL NOT buy finality over an inviolable breach, and the state SHALL NOT convert an unadjudicated classification into punishment.

### Object model

- **charge**: the court-registered assertion that a named person committed a defined A5 offense composing an `offense_norm_set`, carrying the factual basis and the proposed `coercion_rung`.
- **finding**: an adjudicated A6 determination of responsibility (K2 share) at a stated `confidence_class`; attested, settled, or uncontested-after-window.
- **coercion_rung**: the single canonical closed ordered set `{annotation, priced_debit, capability_restriction, standing_loss, custodial_restriction}`. Rungs at `capability_restriction` and above are HARD; `annotation` and `priced_debit` are SOFT. **prosecution** is a distinct procedural step preceding, never folded into, `custodial_restriction`.
- **provisional_freeze**: a reversible, magnitude- and duration-capped preservation measure. It is explicitly NOT the `priced_debit` rung and confers no forfeiture.
- **penalty_order**: the executing record of an applied rung, always reversible on axes where reversal is feasible.
- **advocate**: an independent, publicly funded representative of the person. **special_advocate**: a cleared representative who tests sealed or source-protected material the person cannot see.
- **gist**: an anonymized projection of decisive material sufficient for the person to give effective instructions.
- **matter**: identified by the underlying occurrence or aggregated-flow identity (the composed K3/K5 structure of K2-I32), not by legal characterization; relabeled conduct is the same matter.
- **finality_record**, **reopening_ground**, **live_matter_registry**: the double-jeopardy state, its narrow exits, and the binding registry of live matters used by the A6 consolidation resolver.
- **inviolable_floor**: not defined here. It is the single A9-owned canonical object (`dignity_floor`) referenced by name; A18f never re-enumerates it.

### Invariants

- **A18-I65** (presumption of innocence, soft/hard line): No HARD rung (`capability_restriction` and above) SHALL apply to a person except on an adjudicated A6 finding. The provisional offense booking confers no coercive effect. Pre-adjudication, only a `provisional_freeze` (a preservation measure, NOT the `priced_debit` rung) is permitted, capped in magnitude and duration. Actual forfeiture and any accruing continuous debit require an attested or A6-confirmed finding, or an uncontested attestation after a defined contest window. Contest has SUSPENSIVE effect: accrual against a person freezes once contested until adjudicated, with automatic restoration-with-interest if the finding is not sustained.
- **A18-I66** (ladder, calibration, proof standard): `coercion_rung` is the canonical closed set above. `max_rung = f(confidence_class, cross_frame_reach, floor_breach_severity)`, a monotone, capped, uniform, versioned function computed with NO per-case discretion, where `floor_breach_severity` is BOTH a licensing gate and a multiplicand. FAIL-CLOSED INTERIM: until A10 ratifies and publishes `f()`, `max_rung` is capped at `annotation` for every finding except objective-corroborated priced-class harm, which MAY reach `priced_debit`; no HARD rung SHALL issue on an unpublished or unversioned function. Proof standard split: clear-and-corroborated for `capability_restriction` and `standing_loss`; the highest standard, near-certainty (beyond reasonable doubt), for `custodial_restriction` and any criminal-class rights_violation finding. No custodial deprivation SHALL issue below that highest standard.
- **A18-I67** (counsel from first coercive contact): Any target subject to a compelled interview, a detention-analog measure, or any investigative measure at rung 3 (`capability_restriction`) and above has the right to an independent funded advocate consulted before and present during the measure. The right triggers at first coercive or custodial contact, not at charge. Measures taken in its denial are excluded and book anti-value against the acting enforcement organ.
- **A18-I68** (disclosure, gisting, source protection): The person SHALL receive every read and item of evidence relied on against them; default-deny against the defense is void. Sealed material MAY be tested only through a `special_advocate` and SHALL NEVER be the sole or decisive basis for any HARD rung; the person SHALL receive a `gist` sufficient to give effective instructions, and where the state will not gist, that material is inadmissible for that rung. Identity and identifying metadata of reporters and at-risk witnesses go only to a `special_advocate`, never to the accused, with the substance delivered as an anonymized projection sufficient to answer. Where identity is genuinely indispensable to the defense, the court SHALL first impose protective measures. Any accused-side attempt to unmask or retaliate is aggravated anti-value and forfeits reliance on the affected material.
- **A18-I69** (inviolable floor, dual role of floor_breach): The inviolable floor is the single A9-owned `dignity_floor` object, referenced by name and never re-enumerated here; any narrower local restatement is void and does not shrink it. It is the absolute jus cogens LIMIT, outside the coercion calculus entirely: no bodily-integrity intrusion is ever inside the lawful set. Separately, for the LICENSING gate, `floor_breach_severity` ranges over `{none, threatened-inviolable, cohort-floor, inviolable}`: a Charter Art. 6 cohort-floor breach licenses the HARD ladder as a severity band distinct from Art. 4 inviolable breaches, and `threatened-inviolable`, graded on the confidence and cross-frame reach of the RISK, gates attempt and endangerment without materialized harm.
- **A18-I70** (silence, zero weight): In any criminal-class matter no adverse inference of any weight SHALL be drawn from a natural person's silence. The X1-I14 / K2-P4 victim-originated non-response inference is confined to non-criminal priced-class case-openers, is barred wherever criminal reclassification of the same act is possible, and once an occurrence is classified `rights_violation` it is inert for the coercion `confidence_class`: it MAY sustain the case-opener's survival but SHALL NOT contribute to the A18-I66 confidence factor.
- **A18-I71** (lex certa, notice): The charged person SHALL receive the offense definition and factual basis in advance of any rung above annotation. Every A5 offense composing an `offense_norm_set` SHALL meet a definiteness standard (conduct ascertainable in advance), void-for-vagueness reviewable at A6; classification and coercion are refused on any offense whose definition fails it.
- **A18-I72** (custodial ceilings and review): Every liberty restriction SHALL receive judicial review promptly, within a stated short period; a maximum total pre-charge detention applies; absent a set cadence the shortest default governs; A10 MAY tighten but never loosen these. A missed review is an administrative, not substantive, lapse: it triggers one short, single, non-renewable bridging extension plus a mandatory priority emergency review within a fixed brief window. Release follows only from a substantive failure to re-justify, not from a clerical miss. A guaranteed minimum review capacity per custodial load SHALL be maintained.
- **A18-I73** (wrongful-coercion remedy, irreversibility): Any coercive measure (penalty, detention-analog, frozen escrow, search-analog) that is reversed, does not result in a sustained charge, or is later found unfounded, restores the subject on every damaged axis with the state as payer, on a no-limitation, discovery-based basis, independent of whether a `penalty_order` ever issued. Reversal requires maximal feasible restitution plus compensation on irreversible axes; because reversal cannot make whole, custodial or liberty restriction and any physically non-restorable capability loss require the top confidence band ex ante (A18-I66).
- **A18-I74** (no statistical hard coercion of individuals; frame contestability): Purely statistical or market-share attribution MAY ground cohort-level restitution and organization-level `standing_loss` ONLY, and SHALL NEVER supply the confidence basis for any HARD rung against an individual natural person, who requires individualized, non-statistical, objective-corroborated evidence. The asserting organ carries the burden of proving cross-frame objectivity to the corroborated standard of A18-I66, attested by frames not aligned with the charging organ (V5 frame-holders or the affected cohort); the frame determination is independently contestable before any HARD rung.
- **A18-I75** (A6 conflict resolver): A6 courts, structurally outside the enforcement organs so no apex among enforcers is created, are the neutral jurisdiction and consolidation resolver, backed by the binding `live_matter_registry` and a first-seized (lis pendens) default a court MAY reassign. Consolidation routes to the more-independent or higher-TrackRecord organ, never the earlier or lower one. Contradictory coercive orders on one subject are automatically stayed pending resolution.
- **A18-I76** (self-report finality, strict scope): Self-report finality attaches strictly to the fully and accurately disclosed scope. Any material omission voids finality for the ENTIRE matter and flips it into the perpetual, no-limitation class, with the burden on the self-reporter to show disclosure was complete.
- **A18-I77** (double jeopardy): No person SHALL be tried or coerced twice for the same matter (defined by occurrence, A18-I2 kind and legal label being irrelevant). Finality and double-jeopardy protection attach on acquittal or settlement of an honest party and bar re-charge even on new evidence; the sole exception is newly discovered concealment or fraud by that party (A18-I78). A matter closed WITHOUT charge carries only the weaker standard: reopening on genuinely new evidence plus court leave. Disputed individuation routes to the A6 resolver (A18-I75).
- **A18-I78** (reopening; collusion and buy-out void): A concealment reopening ground SHALL first independently clear the HARD-rung confidence threshold (high-confidence, objective, corroborated); a below-threshold concealment claim yields at most annotation and reopens nothing. Beyond concealment: any acquittal, settlement, or no-charge closure procured through a captured, colluding, or conflicted charging organ or investigator is void ab initio and attaches no finality; fraud on the tribunal by EITHER the accused OR the charging organ (collusive under-charging, throwing a case) is an express reopening ground adjudicated by a court independent of the accused organ, and a clean organ's investigation is never barred or consolidated away by a tainted prior proceeding. A priced settlement purporting to discharge a Charter Art. 4 floor breach is void ab initio with no finality regardless of concealment (the criminal class was never validly adjudicated); under-classification of an on-record floor breach is an independent, non-concealment reopening ground; and the kind determination is independently re-derived by the court, not inherited from the offender-influenced V4 booking.
- **A18-I79** (minors and incapacitated): Where the accused is a child or an adjudicated-incapacitated person, culpability is graded down or excluded per a capacity finding made before any rung above annotation; capacity is a contestable, court-found element the charging organ SHALL establish. A guardian ad litem is appointed in addition to the independent advocate. `custodial_restriction` of a minor is barred save an A9-justified last resort with a hard short cap and heightened review.
- **A18-I80** (no transitive collective punishment; downstream due process): A downstream holder with no adjudicated responsibility share is never restricted as punishment. Any capability suspension reaching a good-faith delegatee gives that holder individual notice, standing to contest, an innocent-holder carve-out, and a substitution or re-grant path preserving their participation floor, with restoration if their share is unattributable.

### Protocols

- **A18-P20** (charge and rung selection): An acting enforcement organ assembles the A6 finding, states `confidence_class`, `cross_frame_reach` (with the objectivity burden of A18-I74 met), and `floor_breach_severity`; computes `max_rung` by the published `f()` or the fail-closed interim cap (A18-I66); verifies the offense clears lex certa (A18-I71); and selects the rung at or below `max_rung` meeting the applicable proof standard. No HARD rung issues without an adjudicated finding (A18-I65).
- **A18-P21** (advocate, disclosure, and answer): From first coercive or custodial contact the person receives a funded advocate (A18-I67). Disclosure runs under A18-I68: full material to the person, sealed and source-protected material only to a `special_advocate` with a mandatory `gist` on anything decisive; witness identities are protected. The person answers the case; silence draws zero weight in criminal-class matters (A18-I70).
- **A18-P22** (appeal, reversal, and restoration): Any rung is appealable de novo; the frame determination is independently contestable before any HARD rung takes effect (A18-I74). On reversal, non-sustained charge, or a measure found unfounded, the state restores the subject on every damaged axis (A18-I73), with heightened ex-ante stringency for irreversible axes.
- **A18-P23** (finality, reopening, and consolidation): Closure books a `finality_record`. Reopening proceeds only on the gated concealment ground, collusion or fraud on the tribunal, floor-breach under-classification, or self-report omission (A18-I76, A18-I77, A18-I78). Parallel or contradictory matters route to the A6 consolidation resolver, which stays conflicting orders and consolidates toward the more-independent organ (A18-I75).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Provisional coercion**: sensing books a provisional classification; pre-adjudication the only permitted measure against a person is a capped, reversible `provisional_freeze`, with suspensive-effect contest and restoration-with-interest if not sustained (A18-I65).
- **Minor or incapacitated accused**: capacity is found first; culpability grades down or is excluded; a guardian ad litem joins the advocate; custody is a last resort only (A18-I79).
- **Custodial review lapse**: a missed review triggers a single non-renewable bridging extension plus priority emergency review; release follows only from substantive failure to re-justify (A18-I72).
- **Collusive or captured closure**: a sham acquittal or soft closure through a conflicted organ is void ab initio; a clean organ proceeds, and consolidation never routes to the tainted proceeding (A18-I78, A18-I75).
- **Floor-breach buy-out attempt**: a priced settlement over an Art. 4 breach is void ab initio; the court re-derives the kind independently and routes to the criminal ladder (A18-I78).
- **Capacity mismatch and beneficial-owner reach (edge e)**: where the attributed actor demonstrably lacks the means the harm required, closure is barred until upstream tracing to principals, procurers, and beneficial owners completes; a settlement with one party never bars pursuit of undisclosed principals, and the natural-person beneficial owner carries the criminal-class consequence personally (routing per A18d). An uncorroborated admission inconsistent with the actor's demonstrated capacity is not relied upon.
- **Silence routing attempt**: a de facto criminal targeting routed through the priced class to harvest a non-response inference fails: the inference is barred wherever criminal reclassification is possible (A18-I70).

### Interfaces

- **A6**: adjudicates every finding, hosts the neutral consolidation resolver, reviews lex certa and capacity, and gates every HARD rung and reopening.
- **A9**: owns the canonical `dignity_floor` object and the conditions-of-restriction minima referenced by A18-I69 and A18-I72.
- **A5**: supplies offense definitions, which SHALL satisfy the A18-I71 definiteness standard.
- **A10**: ratifies and versions `f()` and sets detention cadence and ceilings within the A18-I72 in-text maxima (tighten only).
- **A12 / K2 / V4**: mandate boundaries, attribution shares, and kind booking, the last re-derivable by the court under A18-I78.
- **X1 / K2-P4**: the victim-originated case-opener, confined by A18-I70 for criminal-class matters.
- **S1 / S2**: the delegation graph governed for downstream due process by A18-I80.

### External bindings

- Charter Art. 4 (inviolable rights) as the jus cogens LIMIT; Art. 6 (cohort floors) as a licensing severity band; Art. 12 (livelihood) via the A18-I80 participation-floor carve-out.
- ICCPR Art. 14 fair-trial minima: reasoned notice, counsel, disclosure, appeal, and the near-certainty custodial standard.
- Ne bis in idem: honest acquittal is final against re-charge (A18-I77); the ECtHR A v UK closed-material rule is honored by the A18-I68 gisting floor.
